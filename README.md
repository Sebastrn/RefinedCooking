# Refined Cooking

A Minecraft mod to bridge Cooking for Blockheads with Refined Storage.

Put a **Kitchen Station** in your kitchen and the Cooking Table can craft with everything in your
Refined Storage network — every stored item becomes an ingredient, and water and milk are pulled
straight from fluid storage.

**Download:** [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/refined-cooking) · [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/mod/refined-cooking)

## What it adds

- **Kitchen Station** — sits in your Cooking for Blockheads kitchen and feeds the Cooking Table from the network.
- **Kitchen Access Point** — sits on your Refined Storage network and projects it out to a remote Station.
- **Kitchen Network Card** — links the two, across any distance and even across dimensions.

## Requirements

Requires [Refined Storage](https://modrinth.com/mod/refined-storage),
[Cooking for Blockheads](https://modrinth.com/mod/cooking-for-blockheads) and
[Balm](https://modrinth.com/mod/balm).

Optionally integrates with [Patchouli](https://modrinth.com/mod/patchouli) (in-game guide),
[JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei), [Jade](https://modrinth.com/mod/jade) and
[The One Probe](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-one-probe) — the mod runs fine without any of them.
